### [deviation] 2026-08-27T10:35:00Z — "divergence is the sole reader" is too strong; the real invariant is disposer sinks

- **What the plan said:** `test_divergence_is_the_only_permitted_reader` asserts divergence.py is the sole core module that reads a logit-derived quantity.
- **What the code revealed:** M4's `escape_sampler` reads `proposed_score` to order the prioritized review tranche — a legitimate, non-disposer read that `compute_escape_rate` never touches. The literal "sole reader" is false as of M4.
- **Conservative choice:** enforce the invariant I8 actually states — no logit-derived quantity feeds `severity_map`, `deduction`, coverage, criterion health, or routing — directly across `src/argus/core/**`, and allowlist exactly `{divergence.py, escape_sampler.py}` as readers. The acceptance-test line in the plan was refined to match, with a Decision Log entry. A new stray reader now fails the test and forces a reviewed decision.
- **Revisit:** when 9002's disposer stages (score, adjust, routing, severity_map) land, they come under this live scan automatically; no fixture change needed unless a genuinely new logit reader is justified.

### [discovery] 2026-08-27T10:38:00Z — the fixture is the third instance of structure-over-vigilance in this plan

M3 gave `DriftAssessment` no disposer field; M4 made the estimator's input a distinct `RandomTranche` type; M5 makes any logit→disposer assignment a parse-time failure across the tree. Each replaces "a reviewer must notice" with "the shape/type/parse makes it impossible". This is the same discipline the companion patch's Gate-Checkability Audit describes from the compiled-signal side — a quantity is gate-checkable only if a deterministic check can confirm its referent. The I8 fixture and that audit are one discipline with two enforcement points.
