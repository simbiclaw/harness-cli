### [deviation] 2026-08-27T09:25:00Z — proposed_score is the expectation over the ORDERED scale; M1's capture was corrected

- **What the plan said:** M2 computes the expectation over the per-dimension logits M1 captured; M1 captured "the g highest logits".
- **What the code revealed:** magnitude-ranking the logits destroys the score axis. On a letter scale, position i is score-letter i — the order is the score. An expectation over magnitude-sorted positions is meaningless. `test_expectation_not_argmax` failed, surfacing it before the flip.
- **Conservative choice:** compute the expectation over the first g logits in scale order (no sort), and change M1's `_top_g` to `_scale_slice` so the captured material preserves scale order. The real letter-token vocab-id mapping is a config concern deferred to Q3; the first-g slice is the provisional ordered scale until then. `local_proposer.py` (an M1 file) was added to M2's Allowed Writes for this correction, with a Decision Log entry.
- **Revisit:** when Q3 lands the config, replace the first-g placeholder scale with the real letter-token id list. Tracked against Q3.

### [deviation] 2026-08-27T09:20:00Z — 9002's FindingGraph/EvaluationResult do not exist, so the invariants use provisional stand-ins

- **What the plan said:** M2's quarantine tests assert a proposed_score never reaches the shipped `raw`/`adjusted`/replay_hash of an EvaluationResult re-derived from a FindingGraph.
- **What the code revealed:** those 9002 types are unstarted, and M2 may not write `core/` or `types/schemas.py`.
- **Conservative choice:** define minimal provisional `GroundedFinding`, `QuarantinedFindingGraph`, `EvaluationResult`, `replay_hash`, and `derive_evaluation` in `types/proposer_diagnostics.py`, clearly marked as stand-ins that 9002 replaces. They model exactly the property under test — the shipped outputs are a pure function of grounded evidence, never of a proposed score — and nothing more. The provisional `derive_evaluation` carries a light "logic in types/" smell (types/ is meant for shape); accepted because it is a temporary demonstrator and both layering gates pass.
- **Revisit:** when 9002 lands `FindingGraph`/`EvaluationResult` and the pure `score`/`adjust`, delete these stand-ins and point M3's divergence probe at the real `proposed_scores` block. Tracked against Q2 and the 9002 cross-plan dependency.

### [discovery] 2026-08-27T09:30:00Z — an absent dimension gets no score, not a zero

`score_dimensions` skips a dimension with no logits rather than scoring it 0. A fabricated 0 would read as "worst score" and could bias the divergence diagnostic; an absent entry is honest. `g_used` is the real width available (min of requested g and logits present), recorded per evaluation per M0's fallback contract.
