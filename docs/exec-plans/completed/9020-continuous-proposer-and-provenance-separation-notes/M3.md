### [discovery] 2026-08-27T09:50:00Z — I8 is enforced by the shape of the assessment type, not just its behavior

The cleanest way to guarantee divergence "changes no machine decision" was to give `DriftAssessment` exactly three fields — `demote`, `calibration_injection`, `reason` — with no routing/coverage/auto_final/severity field at all. Structure-level enforcement is stronger than a behavioral check a future edit could quietly slip past — the same discipline M5's I8 fixture will generalize.
