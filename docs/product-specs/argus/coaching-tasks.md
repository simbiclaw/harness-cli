# Argus — Coaching tasks

## User job

Convert recurring rule failures attributable to specific agents into actionable coaching tasks. The task surface gives the agent a focused list of "your top three improvement areas this week" with exemplars from the Best Practice Cookbook expertise module.

## Acceptance behaviour

An agent receiving a coaching task sees: the rule that fired, two or three of their own calls illustrating the failure (with cited transcript spans), one or two Best Practice Cookbook entries showing the recommended phrasing, and a "mark as reviewed" affordance. There is no automated "you have improved" verdict — the supervisor confirms improvement via subsequent fact-checking trends in their report (`report-generation.md`).

## Tiebreakers consumed

- `PRODUCT_SENSE.md § Argus` — Argus does not perform corrective action on agents; coaching is informational.
