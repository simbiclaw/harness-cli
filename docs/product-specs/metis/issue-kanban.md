# Metis — Issue Kanban

## User job

The analyst-facing surface that displays open issues, their status, and their evidence. Filterable by severity, by domain, by age. Each card surfaces the representative atomic claims; clicking a card opens the full evidence view.

## Acceptance behaviour

A PM scanning the Kanban can identify the highest-leverage open issue in under thirty seconds. The Kanban is filterable column-list per `DESIGN.md` navigation primitives; design-doc lives at `docs/design-docs/metis/triage-kanban.md`.

- `PRODUCT_SENSE.md § Metis` — PM outranks BA outranks Marketing; surface designs prioritise the triage workflow.
